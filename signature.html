SETUP

Add your signature

  1. Copy your signature

    Use the Copy signature button in the editor.

  2. Open your email settings

    In Gmail, go to Settings → See all settings → General → Signature. Create a new signature.

  3. Paste and save

    Paste into the signature box. Choose it for new emails and replies, then save your changes.

Other email apps: look for Signature in your account settings and paste there.
